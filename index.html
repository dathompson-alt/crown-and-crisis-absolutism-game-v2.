The Four Pillars of European Absolutism (Unit Synthesis)

1. Centralization of Power

Absolute monarchs replaced fragmented medieval feudalism with loyal royal bureaucracies, uniform tax systems, and standing professional armies.

2. Divine Right of Kings

Rulers claimed their authority came directly from God—making political rebellion not merely treason, but a mortal sin against heaven.

3. Cultural & Economic Control

Monumental palaces (Versailles, St. Petersburg, El Escorial), state art, and mercantilist trade policies projected glory while taming the nobility.

4. Catalyst for Revolution

Unchecked royal power, religious intolerance, and crushing war taxes provoked Enlightenment thinkers (Locke, Voltaire, Rousseau) and democratic revolutions.

World History Reference Codex

The Age of Absolutism & Crown Mechanics

Between the 16th and 18th centuries, European monarchs consolidated authority to transition their realms from fragmented feudal kingdoms into centralized modern nation-states.

Centralization of Power

Replacing independent feudal lords with loyal royal officials (such as French intendants or Russia's Table of Ranks), uniform taxes, and standing armies.

The Divine Right of Kings

The doctrine that monarchs are God's earthly lieutenants—meaning subjects owe absolute obedience and revolt is a sin.

Cultural & Economic Control

Using mercantilism (exporting more than importing to hoard bullion) and awe-inspiring palaces like Versailles to awe rivals and domesticate nobles.

Catalyst for Revolution

Heavy taxation, costly wars, and sparked Enlightenment critiques (Locke, Voltaire, Rousseau) that fueled constitutional revolutions.

How the 5 Crowns Work

  • You start with 5 Crowns and three meters (0–100): Treasury, Stability, and Divine Legitimacy.
  • Meters between 25 and 34 are marked Fragile.
  • If any meter drops below 25 at the end of a turn, you lose 1 Crown.
  • If any meter hits 0, you lose 2 Crowns immediately (and that meter resets to 15 in emergency recovery).
  • If two or more meters sit below 30 simultaneously, compounding state paralysis costs 1 additional Crown.
